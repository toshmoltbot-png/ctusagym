Reminders

IMPORTANT REMINDER FOR STATES

Late qualifiers must have their information into the State Chairman and Meet Director by noon on Monday following their qualification meet to avoid a late fee.

It is recommended that coaches include all athletes they intend to qualify on their original state meet entry, with a notation that they intend to qualify. It is not required that they pay in advance of their qualification.

Late Fees

  • After the 3 week entry deadline: $10 per athlete
  • Within 10 days of state meet: $50 per athlete

Late fees are payable to USAG-CT, and are not up to the discretion of the meet director.

Level 10 Qualification

Level 10’s may qualify one week before their State Championship; however, the Meet Director must know that the possibility of that gymnast qualifying exists.
